Our Services

Integrated support across four service lines

Whether you are a contracting officer, program manager, or commercial buyer, we provide the capacity and expertise to keep your operations moving.

  • Logistics
  • Staffing
  • Product Sourcing
  • Consulting
01

Logistics

End-to-end logistics support that moves materials and equipment where they need to be, when they need to be there.

Primary NAICS 541614 — Process, Physical Distribution, and Logistics Consulting Services

  • Transportation planning and freight coordination
  • Warehousing, storage, and distribution support
  • Inventory management and asset tracking
  • Receiving, kitting, packaging, and shipping
  • Supply chain analysis and vendor coordination
  • Surge and contingency logistics support
02

Staffing

Qualified personnel to fill critical roles, scaled to your requirement and screened to your standards.

  • Administrative and clerical support staff
  • Technical, professional, and subject-matter experts
  • Temporary, temp-to-hire, and long-term placements
  • Surge staffing for peak workloads and special projects
  • Candidate screening, onboarding, and workforce management
03

Product Sourcing

Reliable procurement of the products your organization depends on, backed by supplier vetting and delivery follow-through.

  • Equipment, supplies, and commodity procurement
  • Supplier identification, vetting, and price comparison
  • Attention to applicable sourcing requirements (e.g., TAA, Buy American)
  • Order tracking, expediting, and delivery coordination
  • Recurring and bulk purchase programs
04

Consulting

Practical advisory and management support that strengthens programs and improves day-to-day operations.

NAICS 541611, 541618

  • Program and project management support
  • Business process analysis and improvement
  • Operations, logistics, and supply chain advisory
  • Acquisition and contract support services
  • Reporting, documentation, and performance metrics
